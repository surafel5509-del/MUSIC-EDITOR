StudioOne Mobile is a professional music production studio for Android:
multitrack recording, MIDI instruments, effects, mixing and mastering with
real-time cloud collaboration.

- Low-latency multitrack recording (Oboe/AAudio engine, <10ms on pro devices)
- Piano roll, drum pads, step sequencer, subtractive/FM-ready synth engine
- Parametric EQ, compression, reverb, delays and more — on every channel
- LUFS metering, stems and master export
- Loop library with BPM/key detection
- Invite collaborators and work on the same project live
